# Hi, I’m Nirmal Prince John

I’m the founder of **[Spine InnovTech](https://github.com/spineinnovTech)**, building **[SpineWorkspace](https://spineworkspace.com)** — the control panel for your work.

My background is in enterprise integration and customer success. Across connected systems, I kept seeing the same gap: data moved, but the context behind the work did not always move with it.

An account’s commitments might live in email, its usage in another tool, and its decisions in chat. At each handoff, someone had to rebuild the story.

I’m building toward a different way of working: people and AI using shared, permission-aware business context, with decisions and outcomes carried forward.

> **Humans govern the business. AI helps the business work.**

## What I’m building

SpineWorkspace is being built to:

- Bring business context together across the tools organizations already use
- Connect goals, customers, relationships, work, decisions, evidence, and outcomes
- Let AI reduce repetitive, analytical, and coordination-heavy work within clear authority boundaries
- Let people review, modify, approve, or reject proposals before consequential business changes
- Preserve context so work can continue without rebuilding the business story every time it moves

## One organizational story. Three memory scopes.

Shared context does not mean putting everything into one unrestricted memory.

- **Organizational memory** — shared business context, with governed departmental and cross-functional views
- **Personal memory** — context retained for the individual user, separate from organizational memory
- **Agentic memory** — task state, execution history, and operational context retained for the AI counterpart

The design keeps these scopes distinct, with controlled promotion rather than automatically turning personal conversations or agent working state into organizational knowledge.

## The product architecture

- **Spine Workbench** — role-specific views where people inspect evidence, edit work, and govern proposals
- **Spine Twin** — a context-aware AI counterpart that prepares work and executes authorized actions
- **Spine Fabric** — the foundation for business context, provenance, and continuity
- **Spine Network** — authorized capability discovery and routing across supported connected environments
- **Spine Marketplace** — the planned distribution and discovery surface for integrations and capabilities

The full platform is planned to include **13 role-specific workbench views**. Users work through the views appropriate to their roles; they do not need to adopt every workbench.

## What I’ve built so far

Earlier **IntegrateWise proving-ground workbenches** explored customer-success and business-operations workflows. Recorded prototype interactions demonstrate:

- Context updates retained after page reloads
- Human approval decisions recorded in history
- Conversational notes promoted into personal memory

These are functional prototypes and component proofs — **not the complete end-to-end platform**. I’m now assembling the integrated product and working toward verified live onboarding, source-tool execution, and continuity across supported environments.

> **The goal is not just connection. It is continuity — with people in control.**
